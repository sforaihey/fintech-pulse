## Product spotlight — Alloy
**What it is:** an identity decisioning layer. Alloy doesn't run the checks; it orchestrates them, applies your rules, routes the exceptions and stores the evidence.

**Flow:** application → workflow of vendor checks with branching → rules produce approve / decline / review → analyst queue showing every check, response and rule that fired → decision and rationale stored → ongoing monitoring after onboarding.

**KYB:** entity verification → beneficial owners → individual KYC on each owner. The fan-out, and the linkage between checks, is why corporate onboarding takes days.

**Money:** platform subscription plus per-application/per-decision pricing, vendor data costs passed through; land in onboarding, expand into fraud and credit. Vendor waterfalls (cheap check first) are a genuine cost lever.

**Limits:** coverage is your vendors' coverage; single dependency in the path of every new customer, so treat it as a critical outsourced service with an exit plan; less differentiated in markets with a strong national identity and registry backbone; and it will execute bad policy faster and with better documentation.

**Competitors:** Persona, Socure, Trulioo, Middesk, ComplyAdvantage, Unit21, Sardine, Hummingbird — and in-house origination stacks, which remain the category's largest competitor.

**Lessons:** separate policy from plumbing; version and test your rules. And measure days-to-change-a-rule and files-per-analyst-hour, not approval rate.

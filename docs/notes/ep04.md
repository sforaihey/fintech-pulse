# Ep. 04 — 2026-08-26

# Deposits Outrun Loans While London's Funding Dries Up
**Fintech Pulse Daily — Wednesday 26 August 2026, Riyadh**

## Running order

**Cold open — UK fintech funding hits a decade low.** KPMG's Pulse of Fintech puts UK fintech investment at £1.8bn in H1 2026, down around two-thirds from £5.0bn in H1 2025 and the lowest since the report began in 2016. Global fintech funding over the same period rose to roughly £75.8bn. The money didn't leave the sector — it rotated to AI.

**Saudi.**
- Al Rajhi Capital: net loans at the ten listed banks up 7% y/y to SR3.25tn ($865.2bn) at end-Q2; deposits up 9% to SR3.15tn; aggregate loan-to-deposit ratio 103%. Deposit growth has outpaced lending for a second consecutive quarter.
- August sukuk issuance: SR9.52bn ($2.54bn), up 77.94% on July.
- Weekly POS: SR14.60bn ($3.89bn) across 253.4m transactions, down 10.5% on a back-to-school-inflated prior week of SR16.31bn.
- Context: SAMA began licensing fintechs for open banking services in March 2026 after the sandbox phase closed — a supervised activity now, not a test permission.

**Global.**
- Ant International's FalconTST 2.0 time-series model posted a state-of-the-art MASE score and has been integrated by Barclays, Citi, Deutsche Bank and Standard Chartered for cashflow forecasting and FX management.
- Rabobank has committed up to €2bn over three years to data, technology and AI.
- HSBC and Standard Chartered executed the first live cross-border tokenised deposit transaction on Swift's blockchain-based ledger.
- NETSTARS and Lawson ran a second in-store stablecoin trial in Tokyo — USDC and USDT across several chains, JPYC on Polygon, paid from MetaMask wallets.

## Product segment — virtual accounts / virtual IBANs

**What it is.** One real master account at a bank; many addressable virtual account numbers minted under the bank's BIC that resolve to it. No money ever sits "in" a virtual IBAN.

**The problem it solves.** Incoming credits carry payer identity in a free-text reference field that humans mangle. Give every payer or client its own virtual account number and identity arrives in the structured, validated beneficiary field — reconciliation becomes account matching rather than string matching.

**Providers.** Transaction banks: J.P. Morgan, Citi, HSBC, Deutsche Bank. Newer rails: ClearBank, Modulr, Banking Circle, LHV, Airwallex, Nium. Engines behind the banks: Cashfac, Montran, core banking suites. In the Gulf, typically embedded in corporate cash management; demand increasingly from platforms, marketplaces, PayFacs, property managers and insurers rather than group treasurers.

**Bank/PSP build list.** (1) Generation and registry service — structurally valid, unique, never reused. (2) Real-time posting and allocation, not overnight batch. (3) Per-virtual-account reporting, ISO 20022 camt with the virtual account as account identifier. (4) Pooling and interest attribution at the master — the commercial hook.

**Where it bites.** Beneficiary name-check mismatches when the master is in the PSP's name; KYC/AML ownership when one contractual client fronts thousands of sub-account holders (the EBA has reported on cross-border vIBAN supervisory blind spots); safeguarding of pooled client money; and the returns path, which most first builds get wrong.

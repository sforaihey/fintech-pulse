# Ep. 01 — 2026-08-23

# Tamara Becomes a Lender, Stablecoins Get Charters
**Fintech Pulse Daily — Sunday 23 August 2026, Riyadh**

## In this episode

### Saudi Arabia
- **Tamara Finance Company Q2 2026.** Revenue SAR 707m (~$188m), up 152% from SAR 280m a year earlier. Net profit SAR 84m, down 32% from SAR 123m in Q1, on higher funding costs and expected credit losses. The Sharia-compliant consumer-financing line produced SAR 228m of revenue, with receivables of SAR 3.14bn at end-June against SAR 3.36bn in the original pay-in-instalments book. Credit charges absorbed 27% of quarterly revenue; the Goldman Sachs / Citi / Apollo facility is reported fully drawn at ~$1.54bn.
- **Tabby, same quarter.** Revenue up 21% to ~$104m; net profit down 54% to ~$3.1m. Tabby was valued at $4.5bn earlier this year. FWDstart reports both companies have shelved Tadawul listing plans for now.
- **Merchant-side read.** Checkout.com data cited in market research puts the conversion lift from embedded BNPL at 20–30%; Saudi online retail rose 78.3% to SAR 37.02bn in 2024.
- **Backdrop:** SAMA moved open banking out of the sandbox into a full licensing regime on 26 March 2026, with requirements covering API performance, data security, consent management and governance.

### Global
- **OCC / World Liberty Financial (14 August).** Preliminary conditional approval to organise World Liberty Trust Company, N.A., a de novo national trust bank for USD1 issuance and redemption, reserve management and institutional digital-asset custody. USD1 is stated at over $4bn in circulation. Conditions include at least $20m capital (≥$10m in eligible liquid assets) and appointment of an internal audit manager. The charter would let the company issue, custody reserves and manage settlement without relying on BitGo. Conflict-of-interest criticism has been widely reported given the venture's Trump-family backing.
- **Ripple / Clearpool / Cicada Partners (21 August).** An institutional credit fund that lends RLUSD to fintech and payments companies on the XRP Ledger, with Clearpool building the lending infrastructure on the ledger's proposed lending protocol.
- **UK funding.** KPMG data reported by FinTech Futures shows UK fintech funding at a decade low.

### Product spotlight — Stripe Connect
What it is, who it serves, the hosted onboarding flow step by step, how charges and platform fees are taken, payout control, where chargeback liability actually lands, reserves, how Stripe monetises the layer, and where it stops working (economics at scale, loss of control over risk decisions, market coverage and local licensing). Competitors discussed: Adyen for Platforms, Checkout.com, PayPal, Airwallex, Rapyd, and payfac-as-a-service from acquirers.

**Takeaway:** onboarding *is* the product — and if your acquiring pipeline is a list of merchants, the decision is being made one layer above you, in the software those merchants run on.

*Note: the Stripe Connect segment is a general product explainer. No pricing figures are quoted because published rates vary by market and by negotiated agreement.*
